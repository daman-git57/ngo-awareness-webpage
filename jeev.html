PROJECT JEEV

Initiative to protect stray and abandoned animals.

Project Jeev – In This Life, Save a Life

The word Jeev means life or living beings. Project Jeev is an animal welfare initiative by InAmigos Foundation dedicated to protecting, feeding and caring for stray and abandoned animals. The project believes that every animal deserves food, shelter, love and a safe environment irrespective of whether it has an owner or not.

Through a strong network of volunteers, Project Jeev works to improve the lives of stray dogs, cats, cows and other animals by providing regular food, temporary shelter, rescue support and community awareness. The initiative encourages people to develop compassion towards animals and become responsible citizens.

What is Project Jeev?

Project Jeev was started with the vision of helping animals that have nowhere to go. Every day, volunteers feed hungry animals, rescue injured ones, arrange temporary shelters and even help them find permanent homes whenever possible. The project aims to create a society where humans and animals coexist with care and respect.

Key Initiatives

  • Feeding Stray Animals
    Volunteers conduct regular feeding drives to ensure that stray dogs, cats and cows do not sleep hungry.

  • Providing Shelter
    Temporary shelters and safe spaces are created to protect animals from extreme weather conditions and unsafe surroundings.

  • Rescue & Care
    Injured and abandoned animals receive timely care, food and support until they recover.

  • Emotional Support
    Volunteers spend time with rescued animals, helping them regain trust and reducing fear through care and affection.

How You Can Help

  • Donate food, medicines or shelter materials for stray animals.
  • Volunteer during feeding drives and rescue activities.
  • Participate in fundraising events organized by the foundation.
  • Spread awareness about animal welfare and responsible pet care.

Social Impact

Daily Feeding Drives: More than 50+ stray animals are fed every day, reducing hunger, malnutrition and disease.

Road Safety: Reflective collars and safety measures help protect stray animals from road accidents, especially during night hours.

Community Awareness: Project Jeev encourages compassion and civic responsibility by involving students, volunteers and local communities in animal welfare activities.

Public Health: Regular feeding discourages animals from scavenging through garbage dumps, reducing disease transmission and helping maintain cleaner surroundings.

Future Vision

Project Jeev aims to expand its rescue network across more cities, strengthen volunteer participation and establish additional feeding points and shelters. The foundation believes that even the smallest act of kindness can save a life and inspire others to protect animals.
