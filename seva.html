PROJECT SEVA

Helping Communities Through Food & Clothing Support

About Project SEVA

Project SEVA is an initiative by the InAmigos Foundation that focuses on reducing hunger and clothing deprivation across India. Started in September 2020, the project works with the vision of ensuring that basic necessities reach people who need them the most. It stands on the values of compassion, transparency, and active community participation.

Through regular food distribution drives, Project SEVA serves nutritious meals to underprivileged communities including children, daily wage labourers, homeless individuals, and families facing difficult circumstances. The project also collaborates with restaurants and event organizers to distribute surplus food responsibly while reducing food waste.

Along with food distribution, the initiative also conducts clothing donation drives where clean and reusable clothes, blankets, footwear, sweaters, and other essentials are collected, sorted, and distributed among people living in underserved communities.

Key Highlights

  • Regular food distribution drives across multiple cities.
  • Collection and distribution of clothes and blankets.
  • Support for homeless people, orphanages and labour communities.
  • Food waste reduction through surplus food collection.
  • Volunteer-driven community service initiatives.

Impact of Food Distribution

  • Distributed over 1,500 meals during the COVID-19 lockdown.
  • Expanded to over 25,000 meals across 15+ cities in 2023.
  • Aiming to provide over 30,000 meals in 20+ locations.

Clothing Distribution

The project organizes seasonal clothing drives to collect and distribute clothes, blankets, shoes and winter essentials. Donated items are cleaned, sorted and packed before distribution, ensuring dignity and sustainability while reducing textile waste.

  • 18,000+ clothing items distributed.
  • Operations expanded across 12+ cities.
  • Future goal of reaching over 22,000 clothing items.

Social Impact

Pandemic Relief: During the COVID-19 pandemic, Project SEVA distributed more than 1,500 meals and provided clothing and sanitizers to families in need.

Beyond Food Distribution: The initiative regularly conducts clothing drives across cities and rural communities, ensuring that people have access to clean clothes and basic necessities.

Community Participation: The project is powered by volunteers, students, professionals and local communities who actively participate in food preparation, distribution and awareness activities.

Connected Impact: Many beneficiaries of Project SEVA also participate in other initiatives such as BachpanShala, Udaan and Project Vikas, helping create long-term positive change.

Together We Can Change The World

Every contribution, whether small or big, helps us move one step closer to a hunger-free society. Individuals, students, professionals and organizations can become part of Project SEVA by donating, volunteering or spreading awareness about the cause.

  • Donate food, clothes or funds.
  • Volunteer during food and clothing drives.
  • Organize awareness campaigns in your community.
  • Encourage friends and family to participate.

Future Goals

InAmigos Foundation aims to expand Project SEVA to more underserved regions across India by increasing food distribution drives, strengthening volunteer networks and improving access to clothing and other essential resources.

The foundation also plans to integrate Project SEVA with healthcare camps, livelihood initiatives and educational support so that communities receive holistic assistance instead of one-time relief.
