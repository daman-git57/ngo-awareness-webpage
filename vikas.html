PROJECT VIKAS

Skill development and internship initiative

Project Vikas – Empowering Youth Through Skills & Opportunities

Project Vikas is the skill development and internship initiative of the InAmigos Foundation, created to bridge the gap between education and employability. The project provides students and young professionals with practical learning opportunities, industry exposure and career guidance, helping them develop the confidence and skills needed for a successful future.

Through a wide range of internship programs, workshops, webinars and career development activities, Project Vikas has empowered thousands of aspiring professionals across India. The initiative focuses on preparing youth for real-world challenges while encouraging innovation, leadership and social responsibility.

Our Mission

The mission of Project Vikas is to equip students and young professionals with practical knowledge, industry-relevant skills and valuable work experience that enhances employability and supports long-term career growth.

Key Initiatives

  • Internship Opportunities
    Providing internships in Digital Marketing, HR, Content Writing, Graphic Designing, Research, Finance, Operations, Community Management, Video Editing and many other domains.

  • Skill Development Programs
    Organizing webinars, seminars, workshops and live sessions conducted by industry experts to enhance professional and technical skills.

  • Career Guidance
    Helping students build professional resumes, prepare for interviews and understand career opportunities through expert mentoring.

  • Leadership & Teamwork
    Encouraging young professionals to develop communication, leadership, problem-solving and project management skills through practical assignments.

Creating Future Professionals

Project Vikas has created opportunities for students from different educational backgrounds to gain practical experience and improve their confidence before entering the professional world. Many participants have successfully secured full-time jobs after completing their internships with the foundation.

By offering both virtual and on-ground internships, the project ensures that students from different parts of India can participate without geographical limitations.

Social Impact

Bridging the Employability Gap: More than 30,000+ interns have received practical training in various professional domains, helping them become job-ready.

Equal Learning Opportunities: Virtual internship programs make professional experience accessible to students from remote and economically disadvantaged regions across India.

Career Development: Resume-building workshops, mock interviews, expert webinars and mentorship sessions improve students' confidence and employability.

Youth Leadership: Interns actively contribute to the foundation's social initiatives, including Project Seva, Udaan, Jeev and Prakriti, developing leadership qualities and a strong sense of civic responsibility.

Future Vision

Project Vikas aims to expand its internship network, introduce more industry-focused learning programs and collaborate with educational institutions and corporate organizations to create greater career opportunities for students across India.

The foundation also plans to strengthen digital learning platforms, enabling thousands of aspiring professionals to access high-quality training, mentorship and career guidance from anywhere in the country.
