PROJECT UDAAN

Initiative to empower women from rural and underprivileged communities.

Project Udaan – Empowering Women for a Better Tomorrow

Project Udaan is an initiative of the InAmigos Foundation dedicated to empowering women from rural and underprivileged communities. The project focuses on providing women with the knowledge, confidence and skills they need to become financially independent and socially empowered.

The initiative works closely with self-help groups, local communities and volunteers to create opportunities for education, vocational training, financial literacy and health awareness. Project Udaan believes that when women are empowered, entire families and communities grow stronger.

Our Mission

The mission of Project Udaan is to help women become independent by providing skill development, employment opportunities and awareness about education, health and legal rights. The project also encourages leadership, confidence and community participation among women.

Key Initiatives

  • Skill Development
    Women receive vocational training in tailoring, handicrafts, digital literacy and other income-generating skills.

  • Financial Literacy
    Workshops help women understand savings, budgeting, banking and entrepreneurship, enabling them to manage their finances independently.

  • Health & Hygiene Awareness
    Awareness sessions on menstrual hygiene, nutrition and maternal health promote healthier lifestyles and break social taboos.

  • Education & Leadership
    The project motivates girls and women to continue education while building confidence, communication and leadership skills.

Transformation of Communities

Project Udaan has positively impacted more than 900+ women by helping them acquire practical skills and become financially independent. Many beneficiaries have started small businesses, become primary earners for their families and inspired other women in their communities.

Through continuous support and mentorship, women are encouraged to become active decision-makers, educate their children and contribute towards the overall development of society.

Social Impact

Economic Independence: More than 15,000 women and adolescent girls have benefited from vocational training, financial literacy and entrepreneurship programs, enabling many to establish sustainable sources of income.

Household & Community Empowerment: Financial independence has helped women become active decision-makers within their families while improving access to education, healthcare and better living standards.

Health & Hygiene Awareness: Workshops on menstrual hygiene, nutrition and maternal health have encouraged healthier lifestyles while reducing social stigma around these important topics.

Educational Support: Learning centres and awareness programs encourage girls and women to continue their education and develop essential life skills for a brighter future.

Future Vision

Project Udaan aims to expand its reach to more rural regions across India by introducing advanced skill development programs, digital learning opportunities and stronger partnerships with government organizations, educational institutions and corporate CSR initiatives.

The project also plans to leverage digital platforms for mentorship, networking and online training so that women from remote areas can access learning opportunities without geographical barriers.
